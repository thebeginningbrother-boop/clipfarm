Privacy Policy – clipfarm uploader

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Summary

clipfarm uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @unhinged.clipfarm. It does not collect data from anyone else, and it does not sell or share any data.

2. What data the app uses

  • TikTok access token and account ID of the owner's account, received through TikTok's official login, so the app can upload clips to that account.
  • Basic account info (such as the account name), only to confirm the right account is connected.
  • The video clips and captions the owner chooses to upload.

The app does not read other users' data, messages, followers or contacts.

3. How it is stored

The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private file. Nothing is stored on a server run by the app, and no analytics or tracking tools are used. These web pages do not use cookies.

4. Sharing

Data is only sent to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, to upload the owner's clips. It is never sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

5. Deleting data

The owner can remove the app's access at any time from TikTok's settings, and can delete the locally stored token. For any request, write to the contact below.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than its adult owner.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
